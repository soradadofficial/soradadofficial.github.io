# Night Mode Lighting Reference: Suvarnabhumi (VTBS) and Japan Scenes

Status: 13 Oct 2026. Every fact here passed the fact check (confirmed, or the corrected value is used). **D** marks a value worked out from published figures; no source publishes it. Local coordinates are metres from the VTBS ARP, with x pointing east and y pointing north (**D**, a flat-earth WGS84 conversion of [S1] coordinates).

---

## 1. Suvarnabhumi airfield lighting

**Edition:** Thai eAIP AIRAC AMDT 10/26, effective 01 Oct 2026, is current. AMDT 11/26 (effective 29 Oct 2026) leaves AD 2.9, 2.14 and 2.15 unchanged and only rounds the AD 2.10 obstacle values [S2][S1].

Sources for the table: [S1]. The WDIs and anemometers are lit, but the AIP does not say which side of the runway they stand on.

**Common to all 6 ends [S1]:**
- **Approach lights:** CAT II, 900 m, high intensity (LIH), 5 brightness steps.
- **Threshold lights:** green. The AIP does not mention wing bars.
- **PAPI:** on the LEFT, 3.00°, MEHT 63.82 ft (19.45 m).
- **Touchdown zone lights:** 900 m.
- **Runway centreline lights:** 30 m spacing, LIH, 5 steps.
- **Runway edge lights:** 60 m spacing, LIH, 5 steps.
- **Runway end lights:** red. Stopway lights: NIL.
- **No displaced thresholds.** TORA = LDA = 3700 m (02R/20L) or 4000 m (the others), so the lights start at the physical runway ends. TORA from the TWY F11 intersection on 02L is 3890 m.
- **Direction:** colour zones are measured from the landing threshold, so reverse them for the opposite end. Each end carries green threshold lights facing the approach and red end lights facing along the runway [S1][S4].

**Geometry:**
- All three runways are parallel. The threshold coordinates give 014.41° true for every runway (**D**) [S1].
- The AIP publishes 014.42° for 01/19 and 02R/20L but 014.00° for 02L/20R. That is an internal inconsistency in the AIP, so place the runways by the coordinates.
- 02L/20R lies 400 m west of 02R/20L, and 01/19 lies 2,200 m east of it (**D**).
- ARP: 134109N 1004456E, at the midpoint between taxiways G, H, H2 and H3. Elevation 8 ft (2 m) [S1].

**Approximate light counts (D):**
- Runway centreline: about 133 lights on a 4000 m runway, about 123 on the 3700 m runway.
- Runway edge: about 67 per side on 4000 m, about 62 per side on 3700 m.
- Approach centreline: about 30 barrettes per end (900 m / 30 m).

## 3. Suvarnabhumi buildings, roads and surroundings at night

- **Main terminal roof:** lamella roof, 561 × 210 m, floating on 16 supports about 40 m up. Main trusses span 126 m, secondary trusses 81 m [S20]. Eight composite trusses of 2,710 t each [S16]. DETAIL rounds the roof to 570 × 200 m [S19]. No source documents floodlighting of the roof.
- **Departure and arrival halls:** fully glazed, behind about 40 m-high vertical glazing shaded by the cantilevered louvered roof. The 95%-opacity fritted glass (north-facing, with solid panels to the south) belongs to the main terminal "sheds", not to the hall wall [S17]. No interior colour temperature is published.
- **Concourses:** clear glazing alternates with translucent membrane panels along their length [S17]. The membrane has three layers: a PTFE-coated glass-fabric outer skin, a clear thermoplastic middle layer, and a low-E inner layer with a set light transmission [S18]. There are 104 identical three-chord trusses, and each membrane bay spans the 27 m between trusses, alternating with glazing [S19]. No source describes the night look; lit from inside, the concourses would plausibly read as alternating bands.
- **Lighting art:** Yann Kersalé is credited with "Lighting Art". The work itself is not described [S21].
- **Hotel:** Hyatt Regency Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport, opened as a Novotel in 2006 and rebranded in Feb 2025. About 600 rooms (other sources say 612). It sits above the Airport Rail Link station, within walking distance of the terminal [S16].
- **Airport Rail Link:**
  - The station is underground beneath the terminal (platforms on B2), and the line runs on an elevated viaduct beyond it.
  - Trains are blue 3-car City Line sets, or red 4-car former Express sets now in all-stop service.
  - Hours are 05:30–24:00. At Suvarnabhumi the first train is 06:02 on weekdays and 06:11 at weekends; the last is 23:51 [S22].
- **Bang Na (Burapha Withi) Expressway:** 55 km long, 6 lanes, elevated over Highway 34 (Bang Na–Trat Road) south of the airport. It widens to 12 lanes at its 2 toll plazas. Its lighting is not documented [S23].
- **Street lighting:**
  - Bangkok (BMA) is replacing high-pressure sodium (HPS) lamps with LEDs: 25,000 done by 2023, with a target of 60,000 by 2024 (20,000 of them on main roads) [S24].
  - As of 25 Aug 2026, 30,146 replacements were complete with 15,473 more planned. The switchover is still under way [S25].
- **Colour of road light:** HPS is about 2,100 K and looks orange. LED at 2,700–3,000 K looks warm, about 4,000 K neutral, and 5,000 K or more bluish-white [S25]. With the switchover unfinished, a mix of orange and white road lights is plausible (**D**).
- **Other lit structures:** the tower beacon, tower-top light, high mast poles and communication towers are in §1b.

**When each light is on (FAA / SKYbrary practice):**
1. **Before engine start:** beacon on [S30].
2. **Before taxi:** navigation/position, anti-collision and logo lights on [S32].
3. **Moving, or about to move, on the ground:** add the taxi light [S32].
4. **Entering the departure runway:** all lights except the landing lights; strobes come on here [S32][S30].
5. **Takeoff clearance or start of the takeoff roll:** landing lights on [S32].
6. **Below 10,000 ft:** landing lights encouraged on, day or night [S32].
7. **In flight, sunset to sunrise:** position lights required. Anti-collision lights must be lit if fitted, unless the pilot judges it safer to turn them off [S33].
8. **After landing, leaving the runway:** strobes off [S30].
9. **After engine shutdown:** beacon off [S30].

## 5. Japan scenes at night

### Tokyo Tower (333 m)

**Landmark Light:**
- 180 lamps, 12 of them at the tower legs [S35].
- Orange version: high-pressure sodium lamps. White version: metal halide lamps [S35].
- Switches to white on 7 Jul (Tanabata) and back to orange in early October (evening of 2 or 3 Oct) [S34][S36][S38].
- Summer is white and autumn/winter is orange in every source. Sources disagree on spring (see Unverified) [S37].
- **On 13 Oct 2026 the tower is showing the orange version (D).**
- Hours: sunset to 24:00 JST [S38][S35].

**Full moon:** the top half of the lights is turned off [S34]. A 2016 special lit the lower part pink with the top dark, from sunset to 22:00 [S38].

**Infinity Diamond Veil:**
- 268 individually controlled LEDs on 17 tiers. Colour can change every hour. Running since 7 Nov 2019 [S36].
- Lit on Monday evenings, in one of 12 colours set by month [S34].

**Paint:**
- International orange and white.
- The main deck is white and everything below it is solid orange.
- Above the main deck the tower is split into 7 equal bands of white and orange [S39][S35].

**Context:**
- Japanese law requires aviation obstruction lights on structures 60 m or more above ground or water [S40].
- MLIT diagrams for buildings 150 m or taller show medium-intensity red lights plus low-intensity lights of 10, 32 and 100 cd, with spacing labels of ≤ 45 m and ≤ 52.5 m [S41].
- Tokyo Skytree has white flashing obstruction lights at 100, 200, 285, 375, 464, 540 and 634 m [S39].

### Osaka Castle
- **Main tower:** lit from sunset to 00:30 JST, brightening gradually over about 1 h after sunset. Special colours are used on anniversaries and awareness days [S42].
- **Example special colour:** blue on 2 Apr 2025, from sunset (about 18:30) to 22:00 [S43].
- **Osaka Castle Illuminage:** in Nishinomaru Garden, 20 Nov 2026 – 14 Feb 2027. The venue is open 17:00–22:00, the lights run to 21:30 and last entry is 21:00 [S44].

### Mt Fuji
- **2026 season:** Yoshida and Subashiri trails 1 Jul – 10 Sep; Fujinomiya and Gotemba 10 Jul – 10 Sep. **The season has ended (D).** [S52]
- **Yoshida gate:** closed 14:00–03:00 to climbers without a mountain-hut booking. Cap of 4,000 climbers/day; fee ¥4,000 [S53].
- **Headlamps:** the official site says they are necessary and that it gets dark rapidly after sunset [S54]. This supports drawing headlamp trails during the season.
- **Lake Kawaguchi:** the Momiji Corridor is lit from sunset to 22:00, 1–30 Nov (2025 edition) [S55].

### Ryokan / onsen town (Kurokawa Onsen used; Hakone was not researched)
- **Yu-akari:** handmade bamboo tube lanterns about 2 m tall plus woven ball lanterns, giving a warm orange glow over the hot-spring street and riverside [S56][S57].
- **Hours:** 17:00–21:30, 20 Dec 2025 – 31 Mar 2026 [S56].

### Narita Runway B (16L/34R)
- Currently 2,500 × 60 m; planned extension to 3,500 m [S59].
- **Equipment:**
  - Approach lights and sequenced flashing lights at both ends.
  - Runway lights and touchdown zone lights.
  - ILS and lighted wind direction indicators at both ends.
  - Runway guard lights.
  - A PAPI is documented for the 16L end [S58].
- The source is a 2020 NAA technical report, not the AIP. For layout, use the generic ICAO pattern in §2.

### Shinkansen N700S
- **Front lights:** 2 units side by side. Each unit has 6 white headlight LEDs in a ring with a red tail-light LED in the centre [S60].
- First Shinkansen with LED headlights. The headlight was made about 20% larger to widen the lit area [S60][S61].

### Coach / bus (Japanese vehicle standards)
- **Headlamps:** white [S62].
- **Tail lamps:** red [S63].
- **Turn indicators:** amber, flashing at a steady 60–120/min [S64].

### Streets and windows
- **Suginami ward:** about 98% of street lights are LED (33,309 of 33,925) as of 1 Apr 2026 [S65].
- **Osaka Prefecture:** all of its roughly 23,000 prefecture-managed road lights have been LED since FY2013 [S66].
- **Yokohama:** neighbourhood security lights must be LED with a "white-family" colour. No Kelvin value is given [S67].
- **Window colour temperatures:**

## 6. Gaps

### Unverified
No claim was rated unverifiable. These parts of claims were flagged as unconfirmed:
- **Tokyo Tower Diamond Veil:** the current days and window are unconfirmed. nippon.com says Mondays; a 2026 blog says Monday and Thursday, 20:00–22:00; 2016 sources say Saturdays, 20:00–22:00. The official site was unreachable.
- **Tokyo Tower in spring:** TMG says white; nippon.com and Wikipedia say orange. The winter switch is either 2 or 3 Oct.
- **No flashers on 02L/20R:** the AIP does not list them, but this may be a formatting omission. The AIP says "FLG" (flashing); "sequenced" is an inference. The FTS 800 page dates from 2006, before the runways were renumbered.
- **Stop bars W, Y, Z:** whether they belong to 02R/20L depends on how the page is laid out.
- **VDGS:** the details come from a 2022 supplement. Whether the 2026 AD 2.20 text is the same could not be checked.
- **Skytree obstruction lights:** the intensity class (high or medium) is not stated.
- **Small conflicts between sources:**
  - ATC tower height: 132.2 m or 135 m.
  - Kurokawa hours: 17:00–21:30, or dusk to 22:00.
  - ARL timetable: 05:30–24:00 on the line page, versus 06:02 / 06:11 to 23:51 at the station.

### Not found / not published
- **VTBS AIP:** AD 2.20 onward could not be read. That leaves out the VDGS text, low-visibility procedures and stop bar operating hours.
- **VTBS lighting details not published:**
  - Approach light layout (crossbars, side-row barrettes, number of flashers).
  - Whether threshold wing bars exist.
  - Taxiway light colours and spacing.
  - Runway guard light configuration and locations.
  - Which side of the runway the WDIs and anemometers are on.
  - Intensity step values (cd) and daily on/off times.
- **Lighting not documented anywhere:**
  - Apron floodlight masts (height, count, lamp type).
  - Purpose and lamp type of the high mast poles.
  - Tower-top obstacle light type and colour, tower cab lighting, any facade lighting.
  - Floodlighting of the terminal roof and trusses; the Kersalé artwork.
  - Interior colour temperature of the terminal and concourses.
  - Night look of the concourse membrane.
  - Exterior lighting of the ARL station and the hotel.
  - Road lighting type and colour temperature on the airport roads, Motorway 7, Bang Na–Trat Road and Lat Krabang.
  - Bangkok sunrise and sunset times.
- **Standards:** the ICAO Annex 14 text itself was not accessible. Not verified: the TDZ bar longitudinal spacing (30 or 60 m) and the PAPI transition angles.
- **Aircraft:** airline-specific procedures for logo, wing and turnoff lights are not public. The FAA rules used here are US rules; ICAO Annex 2 was not checked.
- **Japan:**
  - Narita: the Japanese AIP (RJAA) needs a login. Unknown: the 34R approach light type and length, the 34R PAPI side and angle, and runway light spacing.
  - Tokyo Tower's own obstruction lights.
  - Colour and lamp type of Osaka Castle's regular lighting (the sunset–00:30 hours are from a secondary source only).
  - Yasaka pagoda's lighting hours outside events.
  - Fushimi Inari lanterns (count and colour).
  - Mt Fuji mountain-hut lights.
  - Hakone ryokan lighting.
  - Street light colour temperature in Tokyo and Osaka, and LED share on Tokyo Metropolitan and Osaka City roads.
  - Hotel window colour temperatures.
  - Rules for red rear markers on trains, and a JR Central primary source for the N700S tail LED.
  - Whether Hanatouro still runs in the same form.
