# Suvarnabhumi subsoil and ground improvement: reference spec

Researched and fact-checked on 13 Oct 2026 (45 claims checked: 37 confirmed, 7 corrected, 1 unverifiable). Every number has a source key that maps to a URL at the end.

## BKK Suvarnabhumi: ground cross-section reference
[Sx] = URL list at end. Depths in m below original ground.

### 1. Site
- Former Nong Ngu Hao ("Cobra Swamp"): flood-prone swamp and fishponds; ~2,000 fishponds and 6 canals filled [S24]. Land acquired 1973 [S25].
- Area 3,200-3,240 ha, ~8 km N-S x 4 km E-W [S23]; 32.4 km² [S25].
- Original ground: average <1 m above MSL [S5]; "close to 0.00 m MSL" [S21]. ARP +1.3 m (4.2 ft); thresholds +1.1 to +1.3 m (AIRAC 16 May 2024) [S13].
- Why improve: unimproved clay failed under 3.4 m fill (61 kPa), 1973 [S7]; 2.7 m fill settled 0.48 m in 6 months [S7].

### 3. Ground improvement
**Trials**
- 1983: sand drains ≥0.26 m dia, 14.5 m deep; Section 1 40x40 m at 2 m triangular. 0.4 m settlement under a 0.7 m sand blanket after 5 months (section not named) [S7].
- 1994-95: TS1-3, PVD to 12 m on square 1.5/1.2/1.0 m [S7][S9]. Embankment max 4.2 m, base 40x40 m, 3H:1V [S8]; full-height surcharge 75 kPa [S7]. TS3 Asaoka final settlement 1.50 m (0-12 m) [S9].
- TV1/TV2 vacuum trials: 40x40 m; PVD 15/12 m, 1.0 m triangular; blanket 0.3/0.8 m; 45 days vacuum, then 2.5 m fill in 4 stages; pump 70 kPa capable [S2].

**Production**
- West runway Nov 1997-Jun 2002; East Dec 2002-Aug 2004 [S5].
- PVD 10 m long on 1 m square grid; 1.5 m sand blanket between two nonwoven geotextiles; surcharge 3.8-4.2 m held 6-11 months to 80% primary consolidation [S5].
- PVD: 9.5 million m (East runway), 17 million m (third runway works 2005-mid 2006) [S5].
- Vacuum design: ~60 kPa + ~3 m fill (56 kPa); drains 0.9 m triangular to 10 m; target 60% in 4 months [S3]. Some embankments on soil-cement columns, no dimensions [S3].
- EW-4 (third runway, 2005): PVD 10 m, 0.85 m triangular; fill 2.8 m; vacuum -60 kPa design, -86 to -92 kPa at pumps; max 4 months pumping (design); read to 13 Sep 2005; settlement 0.91-1.21 m at 66-80% [S1].
- Third runway, contract 2020: 798,000 m² vacuum + PVD [S11]; ~800,000 m², >8,000 km PVD [S12]; PVD 10 m, vacuum avg 86 kPa, fill 22 kPa, settlement ~0.70 m, consolidation >90% [S10].
- Whole-airport treated area: GAP.

### 4. Pavement
- Runways and most taxiways asphalt, PCN 137/F/D/X/T (D = ultra-low subgrade) [S13].
- Main/East/West aprons concrete, PCN 126/R/D/X/T [S13]. Apron slabs 450 mm, 5-6 m wide, 16 mm longitudinal joint bars; five aprons 458,595 m², paved from Apr 2004 [S14].
- Aircraft pavement 2.8 million m²; ~1 million m² (mostly aprons) already concrete [S16]. Plan: scrape 33 cm of asphalt, replace 700,000 m² with concrete [S15].
- Base/subbase thicknesses: GAP.

**Post-opening distress** (opened 28 Sep 2006 [S17])
- Cracks ~2 weeks after opening, ~100 points in 7 areas; legislative panel cited ~70,000 m² [S17].
- 12 Feb 2007: panel reached no conclusion; members blamed underground water seepage [S18].
- AOT (via IFALPA, May 2018): ineffective asphalt binder + high groundwater; failed after ~2 of ~7 expected years; subdrain trial; PCC being designed [S19].

### 5. Terminal foundations
- 25,437 piles: 15,019 spun 600 mm, 9,647 bored 600 mm, 771 bored 1,000 mm [S4].
- Tips: 600 mm driven -26 m, 600 mm bored -30 m, 1,000 mm bored -42 m [S4]; all into sand below clay [S3].
- Pylons up to 15,000 t each, on groups of 25 or 35 bored 1,000 mm piles [S4].
- Predicted total settlement: pylons 110 mm, terminal 45 mm, concourses 55 mm [S4].
- Control tower: GAP.

### 6. Flood protection
- Dike 23.5 km, 3.5 m high, crest 3 m, base 37 m [S20][S22]; JSCE: ~24 km, ~3 m [S23]. Built 1995-2000 [S21]. Protects to +2.27 m MSL [S22].
- 6 ponds: 4 million m³ [S20]; 3.2 million m³ design [S21]; 4.7 million m³ [S22]. 2 m deep [S21].
- 2 pump stations (SE, SW), 4 pumps each at 2 m³/s; 6 run = 12 m³/s [S22]; ~1 million m³/day [S21].
- Perimeter canal 35 m wide, 2 m deep [S21]; links to Khlong Nong Ngu Hao, Nong Prue, Bang Chalong [S22].
- Internal level -1.25 m MSL design [S21]; canals to -1.6 m MSL [S22]; ponds +1.5 m MSL [S20] (conflict).
